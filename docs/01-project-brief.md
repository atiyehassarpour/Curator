# Project Brief — Contemporary Art Study Group

**Status:** Working baseline, to be reconciled with the owner's original approved project documents.  
**Product:** Independent bilingual platform for contemporary art research and exchange.

## 1. Vision

Create an independent international platform for the study, documentation, and discussion of contemporary art, with particular attention to Iranian art and its place in wider international conversations.

The platform is a study group and editorial environment. It must not be presented as a museum, gallery, or formal institute.

## 2. Mission

- Support serious research and critical writing about contemporary art.
- Document relevant people, organizations, exhibitions, projects, and opportunities over time.
- Make knowledge about Iranian contemporary art more accessible in an international context.
- Encourage professional exchange among researchers, curators, artists, galleries, museums, and other art organizations.
- Publish carefully edited bilingual content.

## 3. Audience

Primary audiences include researchers, curators, artists, writers, galleries, museums, art organizations, students, and internationally engaged readers interested in contemporary art.

## 4. Editorial and institutional principles

- Editorial independence and transparent review.
- Accuracy, attribution, and respect for intellectual property.
- A plural, research-led perspective rather than promotional listings.
- Clear distinction between individuals and organizations.
- Museums and galleries are separate, equal entity types.
- Persian and English are first-class languages. Translation is not assumed to be a literal mirror; each language may have its own editorially reviewed content.

## 5. Language and accessibility

- Full Persian RTL and English LTR support.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
- Language selection must update page direction, labels, metadata, and document language.

## 6. Product boundary

The first delivery is the homepage only. It introduces the project, its editorial purpose, its areas of focus, and a route to contact/contribute. Internal pages, directories, article submission, member profiles, editorial dashboards, and other application modules are explicitly outside this first milestone.

## 7. Non-functional and cost principles

- Prefer simple, maintainable technology and free-tier services.
- Do not provision paid resources or incur recurring costs without explicit owner approval.
- Do not commit credentials, API keys, private user data, or production secrets.
- Keep documentation and implementation decisions in version control.

## 8. Decisions requiring owner confirmation

The four original project source documents were not present in the GitHub repository at bootstrap time and were not retrievable from the current Library search. The following must be verified against those originals before expanding scope:
- Final public brand name, domain, logo, and visual identity.
- Final homepage copy and editorial taxonomy.
- Approved technology stack and hosting/database providers.
- Final privacy, moderation, submission, and account policies.
- Official contact address and social links.

## 9. First milestone acceptance

The homepage must be reviewable in four combinations: Persian desktop, Persian mobile, English desktop, and English mobile. No internal routes or operational back-office functionality are implied by this milestone.
