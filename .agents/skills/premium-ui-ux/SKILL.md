# Premium UI/UX

The product is a bilingual international knowledge and professional-exchange platform for contemporary art, not a simple blog or news site. The approved implementation milestone is homepage only, in Persian desktop/mobile and English desktop/mobile.

## Process
1. Inspect repository, components, design tokens, and approved requirements before editing.
2. Map page hierarchy and primary user journeys before styling.
3. Use semantic HTML, focus states, keyboard-operable controls, accessible labels, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support.
4. Design mobile intentionally; do not merely shrink desktop.
5. Test long Persian strings, mixed Persian/Latin text, numerals, punctuation, and responsive overflow.
6. Every visible control must work or be clearly marked as a prototype.
7. Handle loading, empty, error, hover, and focus states where relevant.

## Quality gates
- Review Persian desktop, Persian mobile, English desktop, and English mobile.
- No horizontal overflow at common viewport widths.
- Navigation and actions must not rely on color alone.
- Typography and hierarchy must remain legible and consistent in both languages.
- Do not expand scope beyond the approved milestone.
- Run available lint, typecheck, tests, and build; report results honestly.

Do not introduce paid services, tracking, third-party scripts, or assets with unclear rights. Do not claim accessibility compliance without testing.
