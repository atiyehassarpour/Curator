# Editorial Design

Support rigorous, independent, internationally legible contemporary-art discourse, with Iranian contemporary art situated in a wider global context. Avoid promotional, sensationalist, or institutionally self-important language.

## Content standards
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, criticism, and opinion.
- Preserve authorial voice while improving clarity and structure.
- Require verifiable sources for factual claims, dates, names, quotations, and institutional information.
- Never fabricate citations, biographies, exhibition histories, awards, affiliations, or image credits.
- Use consistent metadata: title, author, language, publication status, dates, topics, image rights/credit, and references.
- Treat Persian and English as independent editorial versions connected by explicit translation relationships.
- Machine translation must never be published without human review and approval.

## Workflow awareness
Respect the states: Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Revision Requested, Resubmitted, Approved, Scheduled, Published, Archived, Rejected, and Withdrawn. Preserve reviewer notes, version history, and decision rationale.

Use strong typographic hierarchy, readable line lengths, meaningful captions, accessible references, and a clear distinction between editorial content and navigation. Avoid rendering every content type as an identical card.
