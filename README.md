# Contemporary Art Study Group

A bilingual Persian/English digital platform for contemporary art research, documentation, editorial publishing, and professional exchange, with a focus on Iranian contemporary art in an international context.

## Project status

The repository is currently at project bootstrap. The first implementation milestone is limited to the responsive homepage in four review states:

- Persian RTL — desktop and mobile
- English LTR — desktop and mobile

No internal pages, directories, editorial/admin dashboards, or other modules are included in this milestone. Work on subsequent modules requires explicit product-owner approval.

## Core principles

- Independent international Contemporary Art Study Group (not an institute or museum).
- Persian and English are first-class languages with independent content and translation workflows.
- Museums and galleries are separate, equal entities.
- Editorial quality, research integrity, accessibility, responsive behavior, and maintainable documentation.
- Prefer free-tier infrastructure; do not provision paid services or incur costs without explicit approval.

## Documentation

Project proposal, product requirements, end-to-end workflows, and technical/database architecture are maintained under `docs/` as they are validated against the approved source documents.
